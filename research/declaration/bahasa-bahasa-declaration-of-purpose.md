# BahasaBahasa — Declaration of Purpose

BahasaBahasa exists because language is more than communication.

Language preserves memory.
Language preserves knowledge.
Language preserves identity.

When a language disappears, humanity loses a unique way of understanding the world.

BahasaBahasa exists to preserve, connect, and responsibly advance Indonesia's linguistic heritage for people, education, research, and future technologies.

Artificial Intelligence, software, marketplaces, datasets, and commercial activities are instruments that serve this mission. They are never the mission itself.

We welcome collaboration, investment, grants, public support, and technological innovation.

However, no funding source, technology vendor, or commercial opportunity shall redefine the purpose of BahasaBahasa.

The mission comes first.
Technology enables the mission.
Trust sustains the mission.
