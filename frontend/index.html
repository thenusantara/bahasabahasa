Community

Language Infrastructure Is Built With People.

"bahasabahasa" is designed to grow with speakers, educators, researchers, annotators, AI trainers, and builders who care about Indonesia's languages.

01

Language Speakers

Share lived language knowledge, expressions, local usage, and linguistic context that cannot be captured by technology alone.

02

Educators

Help shape learning materials, classes, assessments, and future language-learning pathways across the platform.

03

Researchers

Study linguistic data, document methodology, evaluate limitations, and contribute to responsible language research.

04

Annotators & AI Trainers

Review language tasks, annotate difficult cases, evaluate AI outputs, and help create traceable datasets for future language technology.

05

Builders

Develop interfaces, data pipelines, tools, and language technology that turn research and community knowledge into useful systems.

Technology can organize, connect, and extend language knowledge. The knowledge itself begins with people.

Participation pathways will open progressively as "bahasabahasa" enters service.

Methodology

Show the Process

Data pipelines, transformations, and validation decisions should be documented so the resulting infrastructure can be understood and reviewed.

Provenance

Preserve the Source

Linguistic records retain province-level source information so users can trace data back to the institution and dataset from which it originated.

Limitations

Document the Gaps

Differences between geographic and linguistic datasets are recorded rather than silently corrected or hidden.

Peta Bahasa is an evolving research infrastructure. Dataset coverage reflects the sources and geographic snapshots currently integrated into the platform.

Research documentation will expand alongside the platform.

About "bahasabahasa"

Building Indonesia's Language Infrastructure for Humans and AI.

Indonesia's languages carry knowledge, identity, history, and ways of understanding the world.

"bahasabahasa" exists to help preserve, connect, and responsibly advance that linguistic heritage through technology, research, learning, and human participation.

Our Direction

Build infrastructure that helps Indonesia's languages remain accessible, useful, and represented as technology continues to evolve.

01

Language communities come before technology.

02

Sources, methods, and limitations should remain visible.

03

AI should extend human knowledge, not erase its context.

"bahasabahasa" is being built as long-term infrastructure — one language, dataset, experiment, and contribution at a time.
