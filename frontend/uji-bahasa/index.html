About this pilot

What your result means

Your learning profile is based on your responses during this practice session. The current pilot uses rule-based adaptive practice and an original "bahasabahasa" item bank.

It does not predict an official UKBI score, CEFR level, certification outcome, or overall language proficiency.

"bahasabahasa" treats these results as learning signals: evidence that can help identify what you may want to practice next.
